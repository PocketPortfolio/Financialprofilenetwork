Wealth platforms do not fail AI because models are weak.

They fail because every “AI-ready lake” asks them to duplicate client ledgers into a vendor vault.

Open Portfolio’s public position is a BYOC inference boundary:

- Buyer keeps IdP and approved storage
- Edge ingestion for broker exports
- Bounded context into a stateless inference hop

Pocket Portfolio is the live harness that proves adapters under CSV chaos — not Open’s default enterprise store.
