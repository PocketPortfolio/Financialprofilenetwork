## LinkedIn (4 posts / month)

### Week 1 — Custody problem
**Canonical:** https://www.openportfolio.co.uk/learn/ai-in-wealth-management

Wealth platforms do not fail AI because models are weak. They fail because every “AI-ready lake” asks them to duplicate client ledgers into a vendor vault.

Open Portfolio’s public position: BYOC inference boundary — buyer keeps IdP and storage; edge ingestion; bounded context into a stateless hop. Pocket is the harness, not the enterprise store.

### Week 2 — Redaction vs structural exclusion
**Canonical:** https://www.openportfolio.co.uk/architecture

Redacting strings in a prompt is not a perimeter. Structural exclusion is: what never enters the inference hop cannot leak from it. Architecture map for CTOs/CISOs.

### Week 3 — Pocket ≠ enterprise store
**Canonical:** https://www.openportfolio.co.uk/learn/ai-for-financial-advisors

Pocket Desk proves adapters under CSV chaos. That is not a claim that Open Portfolio is a full advisor SaaS suite. Design-partner diligence for advisor platforms only within CPO scope.

### Week 4 — Not a Plaid replacement
**Canonical:** https://www.openportfolio.co.uk/learn/open-portfolio-vs-plaid

Bank linking rails and BYOC inference boundaries are different jobs. Comparing them as substitutes creates the wrong diligence conversation.
