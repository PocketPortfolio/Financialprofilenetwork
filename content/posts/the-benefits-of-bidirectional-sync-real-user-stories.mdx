## Deep Dive

Bidirectional sync, a cornerstone of local-first finance, offers a solution to this fragmented experience. By facilitating seamless updates across all devices, bidirectional sync ensures data consistency and reliability. Let's dive deeper into how this technology works and why it's crucial for modern finance management.

### Understanding Bidirectional Sync

At its core, bidirectional sync is a process that allows data to be seamlessly updated across all devices, regardless of where the changes originate. Unlike traditional sync methods that might only push data in one direction, bidirectional sync ensures that updates are reflected everywhere—immediately and without discrepancies.

This approach is particularly beneficial for users who rely on multiple devices to manage their finances. With bidirectional sync, Sarah's data updates in real-time across her laptop, tablet, and smartphone. Whether she updates her budget on her tablet or inputs a new expense on her smartphone, all her devices reflect the change almost instantaneously.
