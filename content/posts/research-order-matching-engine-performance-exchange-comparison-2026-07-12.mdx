## Abstract

This research report delves into the performance of order matching engines across different financial exchanges. Order matching engines are critical components in financial markets, responsible for pairing buy and sell orders in the most efficient manner. The performance of these engines can significantly affect the speed, efficiency, and reliability of trades. This comparison includes a detailed analysis of speed, throughput, and error rates, providing insights into how different exchanges handle these crucial operations.

## Methodology

The methodology employed in this research involved both qualitative and quantitative analyses. We collected data from various financial exchanges known for their robust trading platforms. The primary metrics evaluated were transaction latency, throughput, and error rates. Our team simulated trading scenarios using historical data to assess how each exchange's order matching engine performs under typical and peak load conditions. We also reviewed white papers and technical documentation provided by the exchanges to understand their underlying technologies and architectures.

## Key Findings

1. **Latency**: Exchanges demonstrated varying levels of latency, with the fastest achieving transaction speeds under one millisecond. This performance indicates their ability to handle high-frequency trading efficiently.

2. **Throughput**: The capacity to process multiple orders simultaneously was another critical metric. Leading exchanges were capable of handling thousands of transactions per second without degradation in performance.

3. **Error Rates**: A low error rate is essential for maintaining the integrity of the exchange. Our analysis revealed that top-performing exchanges had error rates less than one percent, ensuring reliable execution of trades.

4. **Technology Stack**: Differences in technology stacks, such as the use of FPGA (Field-Programmable Gate Arrays) and custom-built algorithms, were evident among exchanges. These technologies contribute significantly to reducing latency and improving throughput.

5. **Scalability**: Scalability is crucial for accommodating surges in trading volume. Exchanges with flexible and scalable architectures were better equipped to handle unexpected spikes in market activity.
